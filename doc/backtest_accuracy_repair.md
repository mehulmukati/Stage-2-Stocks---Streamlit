## Supported research behavior

The accounting authority is a fractional quantity, cash and FIFO ledger. Prices are the existing Yahoo auto-adjusted research series. These units reconcile arithmetically; they are not nominal historical exchange shares or statutory tax basis. Signals and returns inherit the vendor adjustment basis. No extra dividend cash, split quantity or bonus entitlement is credited on top of adjusted returns. Volume remains reported vendor volume; a fixed share-volume filter is not certified split-invariant capacity. Unsupported mergers/demergers need a reviewed lineage and entitlement/basis allocation before nominal accounting can be claimed. The existing narrow merger registry remains supported; there is no general demerger implementation.

Only a contemporaneous positive, finite permitted close with trading evidence may execute an order. A verified no-trade mark can value a holding but cannot execute a trade. A missing buy stays cash. A missing sell retains actual units. There is no automatic intraperiod retry: the next scheduled independent signal may propose a new order. Sells fund buys at the same close, with all sale proceeds received first; there is no borrowing, external funding or interest on retained cash. A missing mark carries the last accepted price without a time limit, accompanied by its stale date. This is valuation uncertainty, not proof that the stock could have been liquidated at that price.

Costs apply to every executed trim, exit and purchase. The retained convention charges a buy fee on its **cash budget**, and a sell fee on gross proceeds. Thus a 0.1% entry invests 99.9 from 100; doubling then exiting yields 199.6002 before tax. A conventional fee on buy notional would differ slightly. The post-fee target is solved before orders; cash and actually executable units constrain purchases. Fixed sale brokerage also applies to trims. Full exits use the entire actual quantity and close the corresponding FIFO lots. No orphan-lot deletion substitutes for a sale.

Caps constrain rebalance targets. Insufficient names leave cash: two names under a 20% cap invest 20% each. Caps are not continuous. Locked, unfillable holdings and subsequent drift may breach the target; events report those breaches explicitly. The common holdings list remains a compatibility union; `holdings_by_variant`, individual execution/daily ledgers and actual weights identify each portfolio's holdings.

## Simplified tax profile

User-selected constant ST/LT rates apply to realized gains in adjusted research units. Purchase fees enter basis; sale fees and brokerage reduce proceeds. Long-term means strictly after the 12-month acquisition anniversary, including the documented leap-day anniversary convention. FY runs April–March. ST losses offset ST then LT gains; LT losses offset LT only. Losses carry for eight subsequent FYs, oldest expiry first. There are no statutory exemptions, grandfathering, surcharge, cess, filing restrictions or historical effective-rate changes in this profile.

Tax liabilities accrue daily from realized FY gains, net of modeled offsets. Reserved cash cannot fund purchases. The prior FY becomes payable on the first observed new-FY session. Cash pays it first; if necessary, genuine permitted funding sales generate fees and new-FY gains. Without a fill, the remaining payable stays an explicit liability. There is no implicit cash injection or forced stale-price sale. Final NAV includes accrued tax on realized gains, with no terminal liquidation or tax on unrealized gains. Ending a run does not manufacture a tax-payment trade.

## Calendar and reporting

Ordinary backtests rank T−1 and execute at T close. Live Signal retains its documented after-close, next-session workflow. Backtest schedule dates come from complete exchange periods on an extended calendar, then crop to the run. Biweekly parity is fixed to an epoch. Extending the end date does not move earlier events. The calendar's historical fallback is disclosed; only the years listed in the calendar registry have independently verified holiday coverage. There is no automatic terminal rebalance or liquidation.

Headline CAGR uses actual elapsed days / 365.25. Rolling returns explicitly retain 252-session annualization. Sharpe uses zero risk-free return and 252-session scaling; drawdown spans the full run, without annual resets. Adjusted equities versus price-only benchmarks are a basis mismatch, not measured alpha. Gross, after-cost and after-tax results require separately matched configurations. Legacy `Cost Drag`/`Brokerage Drag` columns are sums of per-event cost/NAV ratios; legacy `Tax Drag` is tax paid plus liabilities as a percentage of initial NAV. They are not additive CAGR contributions. `accounting_totals` and ledgers provide actual NAV-unit amounts; multiply by initial capital / 100 for modeled currency amounts.

## Historical identity and coverage gate

`data/security_aliases.json` separates a stable security identifier, issuer identifier, dated legal name, exchange symbol, vendor symbol and lineage. A new ISIN after a reorganization is not automatically the same security. Reviewed aliases require primary evidence and explicit validity dates. Point observations cannot be projected into the past. Fuzzy-high, fuzzy-medium, stripped-name matches and undated overrides are proposals only. All 1,449 saved heuristics require date-scoped review, not just the visibly wrong examples. Unknown/delisted names remain in source events and the unresolved-impact register independently of available prices.

The inherited earliest-event exclusion rule is retained only behind `--legacy-inception-rule`. Every such exclusion is also exported for review. The default preserves it because first available evidence does not establish inception. This prepares the conflict for review without silently certifying the old assumption.

The [NSE Indices rename announcement](https://www.niftyindices.com/Press_Release/ind_prs05012018.pdf) joins Free Float Midcap/Smallcap 100 to their corresponding 100-stock index lineages, effective 2 April 2018, with a methodology change. They are never treated as Midcap 150 or Smallcap 250. The [LargeMidcap 250 launch announcement](https://www.niftyindices.com/Press_Release/ind_prs30112017.pdf) dates launch to 30 November 2017; its 2005 base date is not evidence that its parent/component relationship or memberships applied throughout the earlier period. The [Microcap launch announcement](https://www.niftyindices.com/Press_Release/ind_prs10052021.pdf) similarly supplies no missing pre-launch constituents.

No complete historical universe interval is currently certified. Parent/component histories are compared by date and member, with stale parent histories exposed. Observed component snapshots remain authoritative. Parent substitution and return comparisons stay blocked until dated identities, interval coverage and discrepancies are resolved. Unavailable old/delisted prices remain a separate limitation. Point-in-time membership filtering alone does not eliminate survivorship bias. Existing history can still be inspected under explicitly labelled `legacy_partial` research mode; a request for a certified mode is rejected.

## Candidate generation and publication

Run `data/reconstruct_indices.py --output-dir <new-directory>` offline. It refuses an existing directory, stages all source events/row IDs, proposals, assumption reviews, candidate compositions, observed snapshots, validation reports and an input-hash manifest. It never fetches, installs dependencies, or publishes. Rejected blank formatting rows are recorded separately. Malformed nonempty events fail parsing. Unresolved names get diagnostic synthetic identities instead of disappearing; candidate chains cannot be accepted as trusted history. No page read/startup rebuilds data.

Publication is an explicit separate operation through `scripts.publish_reconstructed_membership.publish`. It requires a reviewed certificate binding candidate bytes, stable security identifiers, identity-registry hash, source revisions, authoritative observed snapshots and validated date scopes. Every scope requires boundary snapshots. It replaces the entire repaired interval, including old erroneous event dates, while preserving unrelated indices and dates. Under the existing publisher lock, source revisions are rechecked, the accepted membership file is backed up, and the existing atomic/conflict-protected writer publishes a new revision. Price inputs must still match the reviewed revision. Existing process/file-token caches follow the new source revision. The legacy reconstruction publication function is a certificate-requiring adapter.

On rejection, accepted sources remain untouched. `restore_backup(backup_dir, expected_current_revision=...)` verifies backup bytes and the price/current membership revisions under the same lock, then restores as a new accepted revision. If intervening sources changed, recovery refuses and requires compatibility review. Keep the certificate, backup and recovery manifest together. No new database or service is introduced.

Each backtest returns a deterministic run ID and a manifest containing configuration, code/registry hashes, input-frame hashes/revisions, exact session grid and accounting/execution/cost/tax/identity policies. Save manifests and result files exclusively in a new versioned directory. App caches check the accounting version in addition to source/equity-policy revisions. Replay cache keys also include it. Existing Live Signal confirmation/checkpoint behavior is unchanged; this repair does not generate or confirm any live instruction.
