## Common execution contract and next gate

The daily and intraday adapters share a tested quote policy: explicit timezone-aware availability, positive price and volume, no future quote, no stale buy, and a disclosed latest eligible price assumption for an ordinary missing sell. Locked or obsolete claims cannot use that assumption. The price and source timestamp go to the unchanged fee, lot and tax ledger; the assumption is never written back as a market quote. This is a research adapter contract; native MoMo and the production app were not switched to it.
