Sub: Special Live Trading session on Saturday, March 22, 2014

 

Trading members of the Exchange are hereby informed that the National Stock Exchange (NSE) has proposed to conduct a special LIVE trading session on Saturday, March 22, 2014 from their Business Continuity Planning (BCP) site and Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) has proposed to conduct the same from their Disaster Recovery (DR) site

 

In view of the above the CSE also proposes to enable its trading members to share trading platforms on Saturday, March 22, 2014 of NSE and BSE under the existing arrangement with NSE & BSE.
