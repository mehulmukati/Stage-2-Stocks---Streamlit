                                   Replacement in NIFTY SME EMERGE Index


            The Index Maintenance Sub-Committee (IMSC) of NSE Indices Limited has decided to make the
            following     exclusions    from NIFTY SME EMERGE index on account of proposed migration of
            Sintercom India Ltd.        and Globe Textiles (India) Ltd.          from NSE’s SME EMERGE platform to
            NSE’s Capital Market segment (Main Board).

            1)        NIFTY SME EMERGE

            The following company is being excluded:

                 Sr. No.                          Company Name                                          Symbol
                    1        Sintercom India Ltd.                                              SINTERCOM

            Exclusion of     Sintercom India Ltd.        shall become effective from         September     29, 2020 (close of
            September      28, 2020).   No inclusion is being made in the index.

            On August 20, 2020, as part of quarterly review of NIFTY                     SME EMERGE           index, inclusion of
            Globe Textiles (India) Ltd. (GLOBE)              was announced w.e.f. September 25, 2020.  However,                   on
            account of proposed        migration    of  the company from NSE’s SME EMERGE platform to                        NSE’s
            Capital  Market  segment  (Main  Board)            ,  the  Index  Maintenance  Sub        -Committee  (IMSC)  has
            decided  to revoke  its  earlier  decision  of  inclusion  of        Globe  Textiles  (India)  Ltd.  (GLOBE)           in
            NIFTY SME EMERGE index effective September 25, 2020.

            About NSE Indices Limited:
            NSE Indices Limited (formerly known as India Index Services & Products Ltd. - IISL), a subsidiary
            of  NSE,  provides  a  variety  of  indices  and  index  related  services  for  the  capital  markets.  The
            company focuses on the index as a core product. The company owns and manages a portfolio of
            indices under the NIFTY brand of NSE, including the flagship index, the NIFTY 50. NIFTY equity
            indices comprises of broad-based benchmark indices, sectoral indices, strategy indices, thematic
            indices and customised indices. NSE Indices Limited also maintains fixed income indices based on
            Government of India securities, corporate bonds, money market instruments and hybrid indices.
            Many investment products based on NIFTY indices have been developed within India and abroad.
            These include index based derivatives traded on NSE, NSE IFSC                     and Singapore Exchange Ltd.
            (SGX) and a number of index funds and exchange traded funds. The flagship 'NIFTY 50' index is
            widely tracked and traded as the benchmark for Indian Capital Markets.
